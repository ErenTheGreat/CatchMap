CatchMap: Fishing Spots & Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

CatchMap ("we", "our", or "the app") helps anglers discover fishing spots, view conditions, and log catches. This policy explains what data the app uses during the beta release.

Information we collect

  • Location (optional) — If you grant permission, we use your device location to show nearby fishing spots on the map. Location is not sold or used for advertising.
  • Photos (optional) — If you attach a photo to a catch log, it is stored on your device only during the beta.
  • Catch logs — Species, weight, notes, lure, date, and optional location you enter are stored locally on your device in the beta. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Map & species data — The app loads public fishing spot and species information from our backend over an encrypted connection.
  • Beta feedback (optional) — If you submit feedback, we collect your message, feedback category, app version, platform, and an optional email if you provide one.

Information we do not collect (beta)

  • No account registration or login
  • No cloud backup of your catch log in the beta
  • No sale of personal data to third parties

How we use data

  • Provide map, spot search, weather/tides, and species features
  • Save your catch history on your device
  • Improve the app using voluntary beta feedback

Data retention

Catch data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Beta feedback is retained in our database so we can review and fix issues.

Third-party services

The app may request weather or tide information from third-party APIs. Map tiles and backend data are served over HTTPS.

Children

CatchMap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission in device settings
  • Export or delete catches from in-app Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Use Settings → Send feedback in the app or email: support@catchmap.app

Changes

We may update this policy as features change. The latest version will be linked from the app Settings screen.
