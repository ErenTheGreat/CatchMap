FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
CatchMap helps anglers fish smarter across the United States.

DISCOVER SPOTS ON THE MAP
• Explore lakes, rivers, bays, and coastal waters on an interactive map
• Search by name and pan to browse new areas
• Bite forecasts combine weather, tides, solunar periods, and species patterns
• See anonymized community catch activity near your viewport

LOG EVERY CATCH
• Record species, weight, length, lure, notes, and photos
• Capture date, time, and conditions at the moment you landed the fish
• Review history with search, filters, and personal-best highlights
• Export your log as JSON or CSV

CATCHMAP PRO
Upgrade to Pro for the serious angler toolkit:
• Hosted Catch AI — chat assistant for rigs, conditions, and tactics
• Photo species identification from your catch pictures
• Cloud backup and sync when signed in
• Trip planner with ranked spots, reminders, and weekend outlook
• Pattern-match alerts and personal bite fingerprint insights
• Premium map layers: radar, heatmap, community activity
• Daily fishing plan, Fish Today verdict, and spot compare

Free map and catch logging. Pro unlocks AI, sync, and advanced planning.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Use CatchMap without an account — everything stays on your device. Sign in with Pro to back up catches privately. Community insights use only anonymized, opt-in data.

BUILD & SUBMIT
--------------
1. Configure RevenueCat keys: ./scripts/setup-revenuecat-eas-env.sh
2. Set Supabase secrets (see docs/PRO_LAUNCH_SETUP.md)
3. npm run verify:pro-launch
4. eas build --platform android --profile production
5. eas submit --platform android --profile production --latest
