FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
CatchMap helps you plan better fishing trips and keep a personal catch log — all in one app.

DISCOVER SPOTS ON THE MAP
• Explore lakes, rivers, bays, and coastal waters near you
• Search by name and pan the map to browse new areas
• See species predictions, weather, and tide context for your location
• View what's working nearby — anonymized angler catches, species, and top lures

LOG EVERY CATCH
• Record species, weight, length, lure, notes, and photos
• Capture the date and conditions at the moment you landed the fish
• Review your history with search, filters, and personal-best highlights
• Opt in to share catches anonymously and help nearby anglers (no name or exact GPS)

BUILT FOR ANGLERS
• Clean, fast interface with light and dark themes
• Fahrenheit/Celsius and lb/kg unit preferences
• Export your catch log as JSON or CSV
• Privacy-first local intel — smarter spots without selling your secret hole

BETA NOTES
This is an early beta. Catch logs are stored on your device by default; sign in to back up
your log and optionally contribute anonymized catch intel. We’d love your feedback — tap Settings → Send feedback to report bugs or suggest features.
