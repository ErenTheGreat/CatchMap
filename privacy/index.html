CatchMap Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

CatchMap helps anglers discover fishing spots, view conditions, and log catches. This policy explains what data the app collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Location (optional) — Used to show nearby fishing spots on the map while you use the app. Not sold or used for advertising. We do not request background location.
  • Account (optional) — Email and password if you sign in for cloud backup. Managed by Supabase Auth.
  • Catch logs — Stored locally on your device by default. When signed in with CatchMap Pro, catches and photos may sync to your private cloud account (protected by row-level security).
  • Community contribution (opt-in per catch) — If you enable sharing on a catch log, anonymized species, lure, and approximate area data may contribute to community bite insights. Your name and exact GPS are never shared.
  • Photos (optional) — Stored on your device; uploaded to private cloud storage only when signed in with CatchMap Pro and attached to a catch. Pro photo species ID sends images to our secure AI proxy (Google Gemini) for identification — images are not stored by CatchMap after processing.
  • CatchMap Pro (optional purchase) — Available as an auto-renewing monthly subscription and/or a one-time lifetime purchase via Apple App Store or Google Play. Pro unlocks hosted Catch AI, cloud backup, trip planner, and related features. Purchase status is verified via Apple/Google and RevenueCat.
  • Hosted Catch AI (Pro) — Chat prompts, species ID photos, and coaching requests are sent to our servers and processed with Google Gemini under a daily fair-use limit. We do not sell AI prompts or catch photos.
  • Map & species data — Loaded from our backend over HTTPS.
  • Personal patterns — Bite fingerprints, trip feedback, and pattern-match alerts are computed on your device from your catch history.
  • Feedback (optional) — Message, category, app version, platform, optional email.
  • Crash diagnostics (optional) — If enabled in a future release, anonymous crash reports may be sent to help fix stability issues. No catch content or exact location is included.

Information we do not collect

  • No sale of personal data
  • No advertising identifiers for ads
  • No exact GPS in community aggregates

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account — all catches stay local-only.
  • Sign in with Pro to back up catches and waypoints across devices.
  • Toggle community contribution per catch when logging.
  • Manage or cancel a monthly subscription in your Apple or Google account settings.
  • Delete local-only catches in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings to permanently remove cloud data and your sign-in.

Contact

Settings → Send feedback in the app, or email support@catchmap.app
